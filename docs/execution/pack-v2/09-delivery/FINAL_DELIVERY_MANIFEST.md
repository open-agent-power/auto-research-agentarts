# Final Delivery Manifest

## Product
- runnable research workbench;
- real tabular research project;
- project continuity and handover;
- plan revision history;
- method/Recipe registry and eligibility records;
- evidence and artifact traceability;
- failed routes and open questions;
- report and reproduction bundle.

## Research evidence
- original data/source provenance;
- code/recipe/model/evaluator versions;
- raw observations and execution logs;
- metrics and analysis;
- negative results;
- scope and limitations;
- next research plan.

## Cross-project improvement / RSI
- experience candidates with scope and provenance;
- independent transfer benchmark;
- baseline/candidate comparison;
- candidate self-modification diff;
- isolated test and sealed evaluation;
- promotion/rejection and rollback;
- multi-generation lineage if achieved.

## Engineering
- source commit and release tag;
- upstream commit/license manifest;
- architecture/ADR;
- Docker/Worker config;
- resource measurements;
- install/deploy/runbook;
- regression and security report.

## Evaluation
- task definitions;
- baseline configuration;
- raw results;
- comparison report;
- cost/latency;
- failure analysis;
- evaluator hashes;
- reproducibility instructions.

## Demo
Demonstrate one real project:
question → plan → method → actual execution → evidence → plan revision → next research → report → reopen/handover.
